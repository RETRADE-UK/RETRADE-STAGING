Cookies and storage.

What RETRADE saves in your browser, how long it stays there and what happens if you clear it.

Updated 26 November 2026

The public site and demo

The public pages have no advertising pixels or visitor analytics scripts. You can use the demo without logging in. It holds edits to its sample records in the page’s memory and discards them when you reload.

Your browser can cache files it has downloaded. Once you have opened the app, its offline feature can also keep copies of visited pages, images, styles and app files in your browser’s Cache Storage. Those files can remain until the app replaces them, your browser removes them or you clear site data.

When you use the app

  • Sign-in. Supabase uses local storage for session details that keep you signed in between visits. Signing out ends that session on the device. Clearing site data also removes its local copy.
  • Settings and navigation. The app uses local storage for choices such as your colour theme, default marketplace and filters. They can persist between visits. Session storage helps the app recognise a return to the same tab and normally clears when that tab session ends.
  • Pending changes and recovery. The app can keep changes on this device while it waits to sync them. It also uses a browser database called IndexedDB for recovery copies when edits conflict. These copies can persist until you remove the site’s data.

Managing storage on your device

You can change your colour theme and default marketplace in the app. To remove local files, open your browser’s site settings, find the RETRADE address you used and clear its site data. On a shared computer, sign out when you finish.

Before clearing site data, check that your latest changes have synced. If you need a copy, use Reports & Data to download a backup or your local recovery archive. Clearing site data can discard unsynced work. Records already synced remain in your online account.
