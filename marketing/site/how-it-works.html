From a good find
to a sale.

You’ve found a wooden railway set for £6 at a car boot. Where will you list it, what will it sell for and how much will you keep? Here’s how to follow that item through RETRADE.

Try the interactive demo

One item, from start to finish

Bring it into stock

Give the set a name, enter the £6 you paid and record the date and where you’ve stored it. It can sit in Unlisted while you count the pieces and take the photos.

Put a price on it

Once your advert is up, record the marketplace and your £24 asking price. Open Listed in Stock to find the set again if someone makes an offer.

Record the sale

A local buyer collects the set and pays £24. With no fees or postage on this example, you’ve made £18 on the item before business expenses. For a posted sale, include what you spent sending it.

When there’s more to the deal

One payment, several finds

Bought a box of games to sell separately? Split the purchase cost across the games. Each one can have its own listing and sale while you can still trace them to the same payment.

Someone else’s stock

Agreed a fixed amount or a share of the profit with the owner? Save the agreement against the item. When you pay them, record the payment so you both know what’s outstanding.

Costs for the whole business

A roll of tape lasts more than one parcel. Record supplies in Expenses and log your sourcing trips as you go, so you can see the costs that sit outside an individual sale.
