Our story

It started with
our own stock.

We built RETRADE while buying and selling second-hand tech. We wanted to know how much we’d actually made on a sale, and what we could afford to spend on the next find.

Two purchases. One parcel.

We source stock at car boots, in charity shops and online. A camera body might turn up one weekend, then a lens from a different seller a few weeks later. We test them together, photograph the kit and sell it in one listing.

When that parcel goes out, there are still two purchase prices to remember. Then there’s the postage, the packing and any selling fees. We wanted to open the sale and see where the money went, even months later.

Sometimes it’s
someone else’s sale.

People also ask us to sell things for them. We do the testing, write the listing and deal with the buyer. When the item sells, the person who trusted us with it should be able to ask what it went for and get a straight answer.

That shaped how we built RETRADE. We can look back at what we agreed, see the owner’s share of a sale and check whether we’ve paid them. It’s the same record we use to see what we’ve earned ourselves.

Where we’re
taking it next.

We’re opening RETRADE to other UK resellers. Whether you sell clothes, games or cameras, we’d like it to feel natural to add a find on your phone while you’re still at the stall.

We want to make bundles easier to follow from purchase to sale and make it quicker to see what you’ve earned across marketplaces. If you’re still keeping part of that in a notebook or on a spreadsheet, tell us which bit. That’s exactly the kind of thing we want to hear.
