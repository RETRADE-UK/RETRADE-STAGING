Privacy notice.

What happens to your details when you try the demo, open a workspace or email RETRADE.

Updated 26 November 2026

Who to contact

RETRADE runs this website and handles account and support requests. For a copy of your data, an account closure or a privacy complaint, email general007store@gmail.com. If you can, use the address linked to your account so we can identify the right workspace.

Browsing and trying the demo

You can explore the public site without an account. The demo uses example records. Your changes stay in the demo and reset when you reload it. GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security.

Your account and trading records

To open an account, you provide your name, email address and a password. You can also add a business name. We use these details to create your workspace, authenticate you and send account confirmation or password reset emails.

You choose which records to enter. These can include item photos, prices, sales, costs, trips, mileage, addresses or names you put in notes, and the names and balances of people you sell for. We use the information to save and sync your work, calculate amounts and produce the reports you request. When recording someone else’s item, include only the details you need to manage it.

Our lawful basis for providing your account and carrying out these requests is our contract with you. We rely on our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure, investigating faults and answering enquiries. If you email us, we receive your address, message and any attachments you send.

Services that handle information

  • Supabase provides authentication, stores workspace records and uploaded item photos, and handles account email requests.
  • GitHub Pages, Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the website, app fonts or app code. Your browser shares connection details, including its IP address, with the provider it requests files from.
  • Gmail handles messages you send to the RETRADE contact address.

Our Supabase project uses Stockholm, Sweden, as its primary hosting region. Service providers can also process information in other countries, for example when providing support. Supabase’s published data processing terms describe its standard contractual clauses and UK addendum for applicable transfers. You can email us for details of safeguards. Read Supabase’s data processing terms.

If you choose to connect a marketplace account in the test version’s monitor feature, it uses connection credentials and saved searches for that feature. Enabling device alerts also shares a notification subscription with RETRADE and your browser’s push service. You can disconnect the account in the monitor settings and turn off notifications in your browser.

Keeping, exporting and deleting data

Your trading history stays in your workspace so you can review past sales and run reports. Under Reports & Data, you can download a full backup of your records and settings. The download includes references to your photos. Save any photo files you want to keep separately.

Clear business data removes your trading records and uploaded item photos. It keeps your login and preferences. To close the login too, email us. We may need to check your identity before acting. You can ask which data remains in backups or security logs after closure.

Support correspondence may be needed until an enquiry or a related complaint is resolved. Your browser may also hold settings, unsynced edits or recovery copies. Read about storage on your device.

Your rights and complaints

You can ask for access to your personal information, corrections, or deletion. Depending on how we use it, you can also ask us to restrict its use, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or ask for a portable copy of data you provided.

Send your request or complaint to general007store@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. Contact the ICO.
