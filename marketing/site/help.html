Getting started

Can I try RETRADE before signing up?

Yes. Open the interactive demo and choose Dashboard, Sales, Stock or Expenses. It uses sample records, so you can try it without entering any details.

Is it free to get started?

Yes. Create a free account with your name, email address and a password. Once you’ve confirmed your email, you can start adding your own stock.

What should I enter first?

Start with one item you still have. In Stock, choose Add, give the item a recognisable name and enter its purchase cost, category and storage location. Keep it unlisted until your advert is ready. Follow an example item.

Can I use it across different marketplaces?

Yes. When you record a listing or sale, select the marketplace you used. You can keep sales from eBay, Vinted, Depop and local collections in the same workspace. You publish your adverts and handle buyer messages on the marketplace itself.

Stock and sales

I bought several items in one go. How do I record that?

If the bundle is still one unlisted stock record, use Split purchase to create separate items and divide the original cost between them. If you’ve already added the items individually, select them in Stock and use Assign to payment to group their purchase costs.

How do I keep track of stock I’m selling for someone else?

Use a partner account for the owner and attach their item to it. Record the agreed fixed amount or profit share, then record payments to keep the outstanding balance up to date.

What if the buyer returns an item?

Open the sale and choose Log Return. Enter the refund and any return postage you paid. Keeping the return with the original sale lets you follow what happened if the item goes up for sale again.

Account and records

How do I reset my password?

On the log-in page, choose Forgot password? and enter the email address you signed up with. Open the reset link in your inbox. If the email hasn’t arrived, check your junk folder.

How do I download my records?

In the app, open Reports & Data and choose Download backup to save a JSON copy of your business records and settings. It includes links to your photos. Save any photo files you need separately.

How do I request account closure?

Email us from your account address using the account request link. If you’d like to keep a copy of your records, download a backup first.
