# Tax, profitability and sync repair — 25 September 2026

Release: 20260925-v1571 (staging uses the `-staging` suffix).

## Findings and fixes

- Own stock without an account was falling through to supplier handling, so its purchase cost could be absent from Tax. Cash costs now classify unassigned stock as own stock; allocated supplier payments use their actual paid dates and amounts, including partial payments.
- Repairs use their recorded date (source date only where no repair date exists). Legacy settled supplier items without settlement records retain the historical source-date fallback, explicitly disclosed in Tax payment details. No historic data is rewritten.
- Mileage tiers are calculated over the tax year before slicing monthly reports; date-only boundaries no longer shift with timezone. The motor-expense choice is applied consistently across months. Yearly Sales month-end dates no longer lose the last day during British Summer Time.
- Tax monthly detail now includes 1–5 April at the end of the year. Negative fee credits remain in expense boxes and report totals. The trading-allowance export lists that allowance instead of actual expenses.
- Tax and Yearly Sales remain different accounting views: Apr–Mar sale-matched profit versus 6 Apr–5 Apr cash timing. The expanded reconciliation explains dates, motor choice, stock/repair payments, partner payments, write-offs and supplier refunds. These differences must not be forced to zero.
- `renderTax` remains the view owner. New scoped `assets/styles/tax.css` supplies responsive cards, reconciliation, monthly detail and tax settings at all widths; the old bridge styles were inside a desktop-only media query. Native disclosure state survives rerendering.
- The old bulk recovery guard no longer treats every queue over 100 records as legacy. Modern entries retain their identity and normal conflict checks. Successful legacy quarantine is silent; durable quarantine and outbox writes must succeed before the queue is discarded. Failure retains pending data. Nothing clears a user's cloud data.

## Validation and release procedure

`npm run check`, `npm test`, `npm run build` are required in both repositories; new bulk recovery and Tax browser regressions are included in those scripts. The Tax suite checks 60 financial assertions plus desktop/mobile geometry and controls, export state, touch interaction and timezone boundaries. Synthetic browser tests block external business traffic. Widths: 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1440; dark mobile also inspected. Browser emulation does not establish physical iPhone performance.

Local browser verification used Chromium 153 via CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE because the Playwright browser download failed in this workspace; CI uses the project's pinned Playwright browser installation. Shared-runtime parity must pass `node scripts/compare-staging.cjs ../retrade-staging`. Deploy via PRs and passing CI; verify public manifest versions after Pages completes.

No Vinted/monitor feature, staging binding, gestures or production data changes are part of this release. The user's actual account has not been loaded; the exact revised personal profit must be checked after the app refreshes against their records.

References checked: https://www.gov.uk/simpler-income-tax-cash-basis/income-and-expenses-under-cash-basis and https://www.gov.uk/simpler-income-tax-simplified-expenses/vehicles . Existing 2026 mileage rates were retained.
