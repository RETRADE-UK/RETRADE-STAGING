## What a reseller can do

- Follow the public tour and enter signup with keyboard, touch or reduced motion. Short phone screens can scroll to all authentication controls. A failed reset request leaves a retry available.
- Navigate Stock, Sales, Cashflow and Accounts without document-level horizontal overflow in the tested layouts. Sales groups orders before paging the list. Totals and bulk actions use the full filtered month. The dashboard chart and cost filters support keyboard operation.
- Recover an existing workspace after a failed page load and switch accounts during a refresh. Tests cover pending local edits and a delayed response from the previous account.
- Link payments, split a purchase, and retain the resulting costs and cash movements. Export workbooks and PDFs using the same pinned files exercised by the tests.

Automated accessibility checks covered 13 signed-in screens across five viewport and theme combinations, plus five public or authentication pages at three widths. They reported zero rule violations in those fixtures. Manual keyboard checks and browser screenshots supplement the scan. A physical-device assistive technology session is a separate check.

## Release and recovery

The Pages workflow runs the repository checks and the browser suite before deployment. A build verifies the public file list, preview content hashes and vendor bytes. Twenty unused capture images totalling 1,452,538 bytes are excluded from publication. The checkout retains the capture sources.

The business-data backup exports workspace records and photo references. Photo objects and the authentication account need their own recovery procedures. The production promotion should include a separate disposable restore rehearsal and verification of the real email-confirmation and password-reset journey.

Production assessment also needs field loading, interaction and layout-shift percentiles. A local signup timing measures when a field accepts input. [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals)

## Public signup decision

Hold broad invitations until the production checks below have an owner and evidence of completion.

| Area | Observed on 23 November | Verification before inviting sellers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First visit and email | The production root opened sign-in. The staging root served the public landing page. External email delivery was unverified. | On a clean browser, enter through the intended public URL, create a disposable account with a non-team inbox, confirm it, sign out and reset its password. |
| Item photos | Staging has a private `item-photos` bucket with a 15 MiB limit, an image MIME allowlist, and policies requiring ownership and the user's path prefix. The production bucket was absent during review. | Provision production storage and use two disposable users to verify upload, display, replacement and deletion, including isolation between users. |
| Partner terms and payout | Production has four arrangement fields that staging lacks. Staging also lacks the matching partner-deletion function. | Reconcile the schemas and exercise fixed terms, profit share and partner deletion on staging fixtures. |
| Database protection | All 16 public production tables and 22 public staging tables inspected had row-level security enabled. The production advisor flagged three function search paths and two trigger-function grants. Leaked-password protection was disabled. | Review the function grants, pin the affected search paths, and decide the authentication setting. Verify ownership policies using separate disposable accounts before promotion. |
| Recovery | The workspace export contains business records and photo references. End-to-end restoration of photo objects and authentication access was unverified. | Restore a disposable workspace, then open its photos and compare stock, sales and account balances with the source fixture. |
| Privacy and account closure | Signup lacked a published privacy notice and contact. Deleting a resale partner or clearing business data leaves the user's login active. | Supply the operator's privacy contact and notice, publish the closure process and exercise it on a disposable account. |
