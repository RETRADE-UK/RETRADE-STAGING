## What a reseller can do

- Follow the public tour and enter signup with keyboard, touch or reduced motion. Short phone screens can scroll to all authentication controls. A failed reset request leaves a retry available.
- Navigate Stock, Sales, Cashflow and Accounts without document-level horizontal overflow in the tested layouts. Sales groups orders before paging the list. Totals and bulk actions use the full filtered month. The dashboard chart and cost filters support keyboard operation.
- Recover an existing workspace after a failed page load and switch accounts during a refresh. Tests cover pending local edits and a delayed response from the previous account.
- Link payments, split a purchase, and retain the resulting costs and cash movements. Export workbooks and PDFs using the same pinned files exercised by the tests.

Automated accessibility checks covered 13 signed-in screens across five viewport and theme combinations, plus five public or authentication pages at three widths. They reported zero rule violations in those fixtures. Manual keyboard checks and browser screenshots supplement the scan. A physical-device assistive technology session is a separate check.

## Comparison with other reseller apps

Resellr describes stock capture, costs and sales as its core UK seller journey. Flipwise offers date-based financial views and exports. Those are the tasks used for the RETRADE comparison. ## Release and recovery

The Pages workflow runs the repository checks and the browser suite before deployment. A build verifies the public file list, preview content hashes and vendor bytes. Twenty unused capture images totalling 1,452,538 bytes are excluded from publication. The checkout retains the capture sources.

The business-data backup exports workspace records and photo references. Photo objects and the authentication account need their own recovery procedures. The production promotion should include a separate disposable restore rehearsal and verification of the real email-confirmation and password-reset journey.

Production assessment also needs field loading, interaction and layout-shift percentiles. A local signup timing measures when a field accepts input. [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals)
