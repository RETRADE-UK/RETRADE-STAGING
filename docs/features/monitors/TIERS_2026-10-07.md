# Match-only tier recovery — 7 October 2026

Recovered from the separate August monitor planning: Canon £0–£75, Canon
£75–£125, Canon Bundle Sniper, Canon High Value. The latter two were names and
intent, without complete model/price/bundle rules. They are explicitly inert
drafts; this release does not claim to recover six complete original tiers.

The second band starts at £75.01 to avoid duplicate boundary alerts. Both ready
bands use the recovered 11-model Canon/Rebel family. Recovered historical model
ceilings: 500D £65, 1100D £70, 550D £80, 1200D £90, 600D £70, 1300D £100,
700D £95. The later August 600D example supersedes July's £95 example. These
are historical configuration examples, not refreshed valuations or profit advice.
Models without a recovered individual ceiling use the band's maximum.

Reported condition must be Good or Very good; absent/unknown condition fails
closed. The adapter now recognizes the catalogue's textual `status` as well as
`status_title` and `condition`. Fault words in titles and accessory-only titles
are excluded. Description-only faults and seller reputation are not verified by
catalogue evidence. Exact full buying/valuation rules remain incomplete.

## Changed behaviour

- Automatic and manual sample scans save only confirmed matches. Pending and
  rejected results do not become finds, review cards or listing notifications.
- The history query applies current recipe revision and match status before
  pagination. Service reads also re-evaluate against the current engine. Old
  evidence and saved/read records remain retained; no bulk history deletion.
- A maximum of 500 listing IDs per monitor tracks source overlap separately from
  listing data. The new service-only commit RPC retains ownership, generation,
  revision, lease, silent-baseline and transactional notification guards.
- Same-owner recipes with identical terms share their catalogue window/cache.
  The global six-request ceiling and refusal/rate-limit handling remain in place.
- `presets.mjs` owns the four presets. Bootstrap creates each once, paused by
  default. Incomplete drafts cannot resume or enable notifications.
- The previously deployed v1.3 accessory/feed fixes were recovered from staging
  Edge Function v10 and are now included in version control.
