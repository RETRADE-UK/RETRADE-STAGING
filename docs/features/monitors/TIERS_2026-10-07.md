## Changed behaviour

- Automatic and manual sample scans save only confirmed matches. Pending and
  rejected results do not become finds, review cards or listing notifications.
- The history query applies current recipe revision and match status before
  pagination. Service reads also re-evaluate against the current engine. Old
  evidence and saved/read records remain retained; no bulk history deletion.
- A maximum of 500 listing IDs per monitor tracks source overlap separately from
  listing data. The new service-only commit RPC retains ownership, generation,
  revision, lease, silent-baseline and transactional notification guards.
- Same-owner recipes with identical terms share their catalogue window/cache.
  The global six-request ceiling and refusal/rate-limit handling remain in place.
- `presets.mjs` owns the four presets. Bootstrap creates each once, paused by
  default. Incomplete drafts cannot resume or enable notifications.
- The previously deployed v1.3 accessory/feed fixes were recovered from staging
  Edge Function v10 and are now included in version control.
