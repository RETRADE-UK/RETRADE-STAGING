## Buying-rule policy

The first three tiers have exact, gapless bands: £0–60 / £60.01–100 /
£100.01–160. The fourth accepts proven two-lens bundles up to £150.
All limits are the advertised item price; shipping and buyer protection must be
checked before buying. They are opportunity filters, not an automated buy signal,
verified resale valuation or guaranteed profit. No purchases are automated.

A named 18–55mm lens adds £20 to the body ceiling within the tier ceiling.
Bundles require that lens plus a named 55–250, 75–300, 70–300 or 50mm lens;
the allowance is £45 above the body cap, limited to £150. Bags/chargers/"kit"
alone never unlock that allowance. Titles saying lenses are absent or sold
separately do not qualify. Qualifying bundles are excluded from the other three
tiers, preventing repeat alerts for the same named bundle.

The catalog expands from 11 to 20 bodies, including 100D/200D/250D,
750D/760D/800D and 60D/70D/80D. Rebel aliases and EOS-without-space names are
recognized. Catalog queries Canon, EOS and Rebel share one owner-scoped window;
only confirmed matches are stored as finds. Nonmatches keep IDs only for overlap.

Require reported new, very good or good condition; unknown and satisfactory do
not qualify. Reject faulty/untested/parts/repair/read-description/wanted/reserved
titles and accessory-only compatibility matches. Descriptions, seller reputation,
shutter counts and physical condition are not independently verified. Missing
battery/charger signals are warnings, not invented repair-cost estimates.

registered account with completed monitors and alerts. Bootstrap still seeds
